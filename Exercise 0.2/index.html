Energy Consumption in Australian Homes

Household appliances are used every day in Australian homes and contribute to overall household electricity consumption.

Different appliances use different amounts of electricity depending on their power requirements, energy efficiency and how often they are used.

Understanding appliance energy consumption can help households make informed decisions when purchasing and using electrical appliances.

Energy Efficiency

Energy-efficient appliances can perform the same tasks while consuming less electricity.

Electricity Costs

Appliances that consume more electricity can contribute to higher household electricity bills.

Consumer Choices

Comparing energy consumption can help consumers choose appliances that better suit their needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Appliance energy consumption refers to the amount of electrical energy used by an appliance while it is operating.

Household electricity consumption is commonly measured in kilowatt-hours, also written as kWh.

Energy-efficient appliances can reduce electricity consumption and may lower household electricity costs.

Larger appliances may use more electricity, but their actual energy consumption also depends on their technology, efficiency and usage.
