Learn about television energy consumption and the factors that can affect electricity usage.

Television Energy Consumption

Televisions are a common electrical appliance found in Australian households. The amount of electricity consumed by a television can vary depending on several factors.

Screen Size

Larger television screens often require more electricity than smaller screens, although energy efficiency can vary between models.

Display Technology

Different display technologies such as LED, OLED and LCD can have different levels of energy consumption.

Daily Usage

The number of hours a television is used each day directly affects its total electricity consumption.

Choosing an Energy-Efficient Television

Consumers can compare television models by considering their energy ratings, estimated annual energy usage and other product information.

Selecting a more energy-efficient model may help reduce electricity consumption over the lifetime of the television.

Reducing Television Energy Usage

Television energy consumption can also be reduced through simple changes in usage. For example, users can turn the television off when it is not being used and adjust brightness settings where appropriate.

Energy-saving settings available on modern televisions may also help reduce unnecessary electricity usage.
